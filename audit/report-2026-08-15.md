# Veilpass contract audit checkpoint

Date: 2026-08-15
Scope: `src/lib.cairo`, `tests/test_veilpass.cairo`, `Scarb.toml`
Toolchain: Scarb/Cairo 2.14.0, Starknet Foundry 0.55.0

## Result

No retained P1 or P2 findings after critique, fix, and verification.

Four independent review partitions covered authorization and privileged paths,
token and external-call behavior, accounting and replay state, and ABI/privacy
boundaries. One credible hardening item was fixed: `privacy_invoke` now requires
ERC-20 `approve` to return `true` before recording a membership. A regression
test confirms failed approval leaves both membership state and allowance at zero.
The client-integration review then moved `note_id` to the final calldata position,
as required by the STRK20 wallet helper convention; the compiled ABI confirms the
order `token, amount, commitment, expires_at, note_id`.

An immutable configured pool was raised as a low-confidence operational risk and
rejected as a contract vulnerability: the official pool already controls the
same funds and is the sole intended caller. Pool rotation would not protect funds
after pool-authority compromise and would add governance surface.

## Verified invariants

- Only the configured STRK20 pool can activate a membership.
- Token, note ID, amount, and commitment must be non-zero.
- Expiry must be in the future and no more than 366 days away.
- A commitment can be activated only once.
- The helper must hold the full output amount.
- Approval is exact and must succeed before state is recorded.
- The returned open-note request preserves note ID, token, and amount exactly.
- The unrestricted mock token is confined to the integration-test target.
