## Critique, fix, verify

The first flow let the subscriber choose price and term. That was not a creator
offer: a modified client could request cheap or extended access.

The fix adds:

- creator-generated offer links with a random offer nonce;
- `Poseidon(creator, amount, term, nonce)` recorded by the helper;
- on-chain activation time and exact publisher-side duration verification;
- a wallet-resolved note ID stored with each membership;
- global access-commitment and note-ID replay rejection;
- a publisher verifier that refuses access unless the creator privately confirms
  receipt of the expected note, token, and amount.

Contract and client tests cover these boundaries. Mainnet behavior remains an
open gate until an authorized wallet completes declaration, deployment, and
real STRK20 transactions.
